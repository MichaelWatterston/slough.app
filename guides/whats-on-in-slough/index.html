What's on in Slough

Where events happen, what you can do any week of the year, and how to find out what your neighbours are up to this weekend.

Last checked: 26 November 2026

The short version

  • Slough has no single events calendar, so this page maps out where things happen and when in the year to expect them.
  • Small local events, such as school fairs, community meals, sports sessions and group meet-ups, are posted by residents in the Slough App feed and groups.
  • We do not list dates we cannot confirm. Always check with the organiser before you travel.

Things you can do in Slough any week

  • Skate at Slough Ice Arena. The rink on Montem Lane runs public sessions and lessons, and hosts ice hockey.
  • Run, jog or walk Upton Court parkrun. A free, timed 5k in Upton Court Park on Saturday mornings.
  • Borrow, study or catch a show at The Curve. The town centre library on William Street also has a performance space and rooms for classes.
  • Bowl or bounce at Salt Hill Activity Centre. Indoor activities including ten-pin bowling, trampolines and soft play, next to Salt Hill Park.
  • Watch Slough Town FC. The Rebels play home games at Arbour Park on Stoke Road.
  • Walk Herschel Park. A restored Victorian park and nature reserve a short walk from the High Street.

Slough's year, season by season

Dates change every year, and several festivals follow lunar calendars, so treat this as a guide to when to start looking.

Spring

Vaisakhi in April is marked by Slough's Sikh community. Easter brings school holiday activities at the leisure centres and libraries. Eid celebrations move through the calendar by around eleven days each year, so check the date before planning.

Summer

The parks do the heavy lifting: funfairs, sports days, community fun days and school summer fairs in June and July. The six-week school holiday is the busiest time for children's activities at The Curve and the leisure centres.

Autumn

Diwali falls in October or November and lights up homes, temples and restaurants across town. Bonfire Night displays follow in early November, then Remembrance Sunday services.

Winter

The town centre Christmas lights switch-on has been held in November in recent years, with a stage, market stalls and entertainment on the High Street. December adds school and church carol services, and the ice arena is at its busiest.

Free things to do in Slough

  • The Jubilee River path. A flat walking and cycling route along the flood relief channel on the southern edge of town, good for spotting herons and kingfishers.
  • The Slough Arm of the Grand Union Canal. A quiet towpath walk that starts near Stoke Road.
  • Herschel Park and Upton Court Park. Open every day, with play areas and plenty of space.
  • The Curve. Free to walk in, with free library membership and regular free sessions for children.
  • Meet Station Jim. The preserved Victorian charity dog stands in a glass case on platform 5 at Slough station.

Ten minutes further

Slough is well placed for days out. Windsor is six minutes away on the branch line from Slough station to Windsor & Eton Central. Black Park and Langley Park, two country parks run by Buckinghamshire Council, sit just north of Langley. The Elizabeth line runs from Slough into central London without a change.

How to find out what's on this weekend

  1. Open the Slough App feed. Residents, groups and local organisers post events here first, including the small ones.
  2. Join a group for your interest. There are groups across 14 categories, including sport, families, food and faith.
  3. Check the venue directly. The Curve, the ice arena and Slough Town FC all publish their own timetables and fixtures.
  4. Look at ticket sites last. Their "Slough" pages often include events in Maidenhead, Windsor and London, so check the address.

Running something yourself? Post it in the app for free so your neighbours see it.

Slough fun facts: showbiz and stargazing

Four reasons Slough has had more time in the spotlight than people expect.

  • The world's biggest telescope stood in a Slough gardenWilliam Herschel built a 40-foot telescope behind his house on Windsor Road. It was the largest in the world for 50 years, and with it he discovered two of Saturn's moons, Mimas and Enceladus, in 1789.
  • A stuffed dog has watched platform 5 since 1896Station Jim collected coins for railway widows and orphans in a tin strapped to his harness. When he died in 1896 he was preserved, and he is still at Slough station. He was restored and given a new cabinet in 2025.
  • Thunderbirds was filmed on the Trading EstateGerry Anderson's puppet studios were on Slough Trading Estate in the 1960s. Thunderbirds, Stingray and Captain Scarlet were all filmed there.
  • The Office made Slough a TV landmarkThe BBC comedy The Office, first shown in 2001, is set at the Slough branch of the fictional paper company Wernham Hogg.

What's on in Slough: common questions

What is there to do in Slough this weekend?

Regular options include public skating at Slough Ice Arena, the free Upton Court parkrun on Saturday morning, bowling and trampolines at Salt Hill Activity Centre, events at The Curve and Slough Town FC home games at Arbour Park. For one-off local events, check the Slough App feed and groups, where residents and organisers post what is happening.

What free things can you do in Slough?

Free options include walking or cycling the Jubilee River path, the towpath of the Slough Arm of the Grand Union Canal, Herschel Park, Upton Court Park, the library at The Curve, and seeing Station Jim, the preserved Victorian charity dog on platform 5 at Slough station.

Where are events held in Slough?

The main venues are The Curve in the town centre, Upton Court Park for large outdoor events, the High Street for markets and the Christmas lights switch-on, Slough Ice Arena, Salt Hill Park and Arbour Park. Many community events are held in community centres and places of worship.

When is the Slough Christmas lights switch-on?

It has been held in the town centre in November in recent years. The date changes each year, so check with the organisers or the Slough App feed for the confirmed date.

What is there to do near Slough?

Windsor is six minutes away by train from Slough station. Black Park and Langley Park country parks are just north of Langley, and the Elizabeth line runs direct from Slough to central London.

How do I promote a local event in Slough?

You can post your event for free in the Slough App, in the local feed or in a relevant group, so people who live in Slough see it.
