Slough fun facts: the basics

Three to start with. Every guide has its own set.

  • Slough used to be in BuckinghamshireThe town only became part of Berkshire in 1974, in that year's local government reorganisation.
  • The name is more than 800 years oldSlough is first recorded in the 1190s, written as "Slo".
  • It is home to a record-breaking business parkSlough Trading Estate is described as the largest industrial estate in single private ownership in Europe.

About these guides

Who writes the Slough guides?

The guides are written by the Slough App team, using information published by Slough Borough Council, National Rail and local venues. Each guide shows the date it was last checked.

Are these official council pages?

No. Slough App is an independent community app and is not run by Slough Borough Council. Each guide links to the official source, and the official source is right if the two ever differ.

Can I suggest a guide?

Yes. Tell us what you would like covered through the Help and Contact page, or post it in the Slough App.

Sources

Guides draw on Slough Borough Council, National Rail and local venues. Slough App is not run by the council.
