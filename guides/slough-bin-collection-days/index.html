Slough bin collection days

How to find your bin day, what goes in which bin, the Christmas 2026 changes and what the extra services cost, all on one page.

Last checked: 26 November 2026

The short version

  • Grey, red and green bins are each emptied once every two weeks, and they are not all collected in the same week.
  • Your day and week depend on your road. Look it up in the council's Find my bin day directory.
  • Bins need to be out by 6am on collection day.
  • Christmas and New Year 2026/27 collections move. The revised days are below.

How to find your bin day in Slough

Slough Borough Council does not publish one calendar for the whole town, because the schedule is set road by road. There are two official ways to check yours.

  1. The bin day directory. Open Find my bin day and search for your road. The result tells you your collection day and which bin goes out in which week.
  2. The Waste and Recycling Portal. Sign in at waste.slough.gov.uk to see your collections, report a missed bin and manage a garden waste subscription.

The directory search is fussy, so three tips from the council save a failed search:

  • Type one keyword from the road name, for example "Bath" for Bath Road.
  • Leave out your house number or house name.
  • Make sure there is no space after the road name.

What goes in each Slough bin

Slough household bins at a glance
BinPut inKeep out
Grey bin (rubbish) General rubbish, crisp packets, plastic film, polystyrene, disposable nappies, bagged pet waste. Food waste, which goes in the silver caddy. Anything that can go in the red bin.
Red bin (recycling) Rinsed drink cans, food tins, glass bottles and jars with their lids, paper, card, plastic bottles, pots and tubs. Bin liners, plastic bags, cling film, nappies, polystyrene, takeaway containers, toys, garden or food waste.
Green bin (garden waste) Grass cuttings, leaves, hedge trimmings, small plants and weeds, bark, twigs and small branches. Kitchen, food or pet waste, bin liners, glass and plastics.
Silver caddy (food waste) Food waste. Flats may have a shared communal food bin instead. Packaging. See the council's food waste page for the full list.

A bin filled with the wrong items will not be emptied. The crew leaves a flyer on the handle saying why. Bins weighing more than 30kg are also left, and so are extra bags placed beside the bin.

Christmas and New Year 2026/27 bin days

Collections scheduled from Monday 21 to Thursday 24 December 2026 happen as normal. From Christmas Day they run late, and they do not catch up until the week of 18 January 2027. The changes cover wheelie bins and food waste.

What to do if your bin is missed

  • Wait until after 7pm on your collection day, then report it within two working days.
  • Report grey, red, green and communal bins through the Waste and Recycling Portal. Food waste has its own missed collection form.
  • If you have already reported it, do not report it again. Leave the bin out for the next scheduled collection.

Before reporting, check the handle for a flyer. A bin that was rejected for the wrong contents is not counted as missed.

Before you pay £54, try giving it away

A sofa, wardrobe or bike that still works is often wanted by someone a few streets away. Listing it for free on the Slough App marketplace costs nothing, the person collects it, and it stays out of the waste stream. If nobody takes it within a few days, the council collection is still there.

Chalvey tip opening hours

Chalvey Household Waste Recycling Centre is open seven days a week and takes garden waste free of charge.

  • Winter, 1 October to 31 March: 9am to 5pm.
  • Summer, 1 April to 30 September: 9am to 6pm.
  • Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve: 9am to 1pm.
  • Closed: Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day.

Last entry is 15 minutes before closing, and the site sometimes shuts briefly while containers are swapped. Check the council's recycling centre page for access rules before you load the car.

Slough fun facts: rubbish edition

Three things about Slough and waste that are worth knowing while you wheel the bin out.

  • The wheelie bin was born in SloughFrank Rotherham Mouldings, a Slough plastics firm, is widely credited with making the first modern wheelie bin in March 1968. It was built to move waste around the factory floor. Councils only started handing them to households in the 1980s.
  • The Trading Estate started out as "The Dump"Slough Trading Estate began as a First World War depot for repairing military vehicles. So many broken lorries piled up there that locals nicknamed it The Dump. It was sold in 1920 and turned into the business estate.
  • The first Mars bar was made hereForrest Mars set up in a rented factory on the Trading Estate, and the first Mars bars were made by hand in Slough in 1932. The wrapper goes in the grey bin.

Slough bin questions

What day is my bin collection in Slough?

It depends on your road. Search for your road name in Slough Borough Council's Find my bin day directory, or sign in to the Waste and Recycling Portal at waste.slough.gov.uk. Leave out your house number when you search.

How often are bins collected in Slough?

Grey rubbish bins, red recycling bins and green garden waste bins are each collected every two weeks. They are not all collected in the same week, so the directory tells you which bin goes out in which week.

What time should I put my bin out in Slough?

Bins should be ready for collection by 6am on your collection day.

Is garden waste collection free in Slough?

No. The green bin is collected by annual subscription only, which costs £55 a year for 25 fortnightly collections. The bin itself costs £52. Chalvey Household Waste Recycling Centre accepts garden waste free of charge.

How do I report a missed bin in Slough?

Report it after 7pm on your collection day and within two working days, using the council's Waste and Recycling Portal. Missed food waste collections have a separate online form.

When are bins collected over Christmas 2026 in Slough?

Collections from Monday 21 to Thursday 24 December 2026 are unchanged. From Friday 25 December collections run later than normal, for example Friday 25 December moves to Tuesday 29 December, and the schedule stays shifted until Saturday 16 January 2027.
