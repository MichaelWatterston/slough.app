Christmas in Slough 2026

The rail closure, the bin changes, the tip hours and the school dates, plus a few things to do, so nothing catches you out between now and January.

Last checked: 26 November 2026

The short version

  • Trains: none from Slough on Christmas Day or Boxing Day, and none between Slough and London Paddington from Sunday 27 December to Sunday 3 January.
  • Bins: collections due from Christmas Day onwards run late until mid January.
  • Schools: council-calendar schools finish on Friday 18 December and go back on Monday 4 January.
  • The tip: closed on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day, with a 1pm finish on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.

Trains from Slough over Christmas 2026

This is the one to plan around. Network Rail is rebuilding Dolphin Junction, the set of points near Slough, and the line into London Paddington is shut for more than a week. If you commute, are flying from Heathrow, or have family arriving by train, check these dates first.

The Windsor branch still runs

Trains between Slough and Windsor & Eton Central run normally from 27 December to 3 January. They do not run on Christmas Day or Boxing Day.

A way into London that avoids the buses

GWR says its tickets are accepted on South Western Railway between Windsor & Eton Riverside and London Waterloo during the closure. So you can take the branch line to Windsor & Eton Central, walk across town to Windsor & Eton Riverside and catch a Waterloo train. Check times before you set off, and confirm your ticket is valid on the National Rail Christmas page.

Getting to Heathrow

From 27 to 30 December the Piccadilly line is the only train between central London and Heathrow. From Slough, local buses to the airport are likely to be the simpler option on those days.

Bank holidays this Christmas

  • Christmas Day: Friday 25 December 2026.
  • Boxing Day: Saturday 26 December, with the bank holiday moved to Monday 28 December.
  • New Year's Day: Friday 1 January 2027.

Chalvey tip Christmas opening hours

  • Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December: 9am to 1pm.
  • Christmas Day and Boxing Day: closed.
  • Sunday 27 to Wednesday 30 December: 9am to 5pm, the normal winter hours.
  • New Year's Eve, Thursday 31 December: 9am to 1pm.
  • New Year's Day: closed.

Last entry is 15 minutes before closing. The days after Christmas are busy, so go early.

School Christmas holidays in Slough

For community and voluntary controlled schools, the last day of term is Friday 18 December 2026 and pupils return on Monday 4 January 2027. Academies, free schools and voluntary aided schools can differ by a day or two, and some schools start January with an INSET day. Full dates are in our school term dates guide.

Christmas things to do in and around Slough

  • Windsor Great Park Illuminated. A 2.2km light trail running from 13 November 2026 to 3 January 2027, with timed tickets and entry from the Savill Garden car park. It is a short drive from Slough.
  • Windsor at Christmas. Six minutes on the branch line, with the castle, shops and lights. The branch runs every day except Christmas Day and Boxing Day.
  • Ice skating in Slough. Slough Ice Arena is an indoor rink, so there is skating whatever the weather. Check its timetable for sessions over the holidays.
  • Slough town centre. The Christmas lights switch-on is run by local organisers and has been held on a November Saturday in recent years. No 2026 date was published on their websites when we checked.
  • School fairs, carol services and community meals. These are the events that rarely make it onto ticket sites. Residents post them in the Slough App feed and groups.

For venues and year-round ideas, see what's on in Slough.

After Christmas: pass it on

Duplicate presents, outgrown bikes and the old TV all have a taker somewhere in Slough. List them on the Slough App marketplace, for sale or for free, and they are collected by someone local. It beats paying for a bulky waste collection in January.

If Christmas is a hard time

Not everyone has a full house. Community groups and places of worship across Slough put on shared meals and get-togethers over the holidays, and neighbours post them in the app. If you are struggling, Samaritans are free to call on 116 123 at any time, including Christmas Day.

Slough fun facts: festive edition

Four for the Christmas dinner table.

  • A family once sang inside a telescope in SloughWilliam Herschel's 40-foot telescope was taken down at the end of 1839. To mark the occasion, the Herschel family gathered inside the giant tube and sang a song.
  • A New Year's Day murderer was caught by telegraph from SloughOn 1 January 1845 John Tawell fled a murder at Salt Hill by boarding a train at Slough for Paddington. A telegraph message got there first, and he was arrested the next day. It is believed to be the first time the telegraph helped catch a murderer. The machine had no letter Q, so the message described him as dressed like a "kwaker".
  • England's first known Christmas tree stood in WindsorQueen Charlotte set up the first known English Christmas tree in December 1800, at Queen's Lodge in Windsor, for a children's party. Windsor is the next town south of Slough.
  • Slough made the nation's bedtime drinkThe Horlicks factory in Slough was completed in 1908 at a cost of £28,000. The building has since been restored as homes in the Horlicks Quarter.

Christmas in Slough: common questions

Are trains running from Slough on Christmas Day and Boxing Day 2026?

No. There are no trains on Christmas Day, Friday 25 December 2026, and no GWR or Elizabeth line trains on Boxing Day, Saturday 26 December 2026.

Are trains running between Slough and London Paddington after Christmas 2026?

No. Because of engineering work at Dolphin Junction, no trains run between Slough and London Paddington from Sunday 27 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. Replacement buses run from Slough, and GWR runs an amended service between Slough and Reading. Normal service is expected from Monday 4 January 2027.

Is the Slough to Windsor train running over Christmas 2026?

Yes, except on Christmas Day and Boxing Day. Trains between Slough and Windsor & Eton Central run normally from 27 December 2026 to 3 January 2027.

When are bins collected in Slough over Christmas 2026?

Collections from Monday 21 to Thursday 24 December are unchanged. Christmas Day collections move to Tuesday 29 December, Monday 28 December moves to Wednesday 30 December, and collections stay later than normal until Saturday 16 January 2027.

Is Chalvey tip open over Christmas?

Chalvey Household Waste Recycling Centre is closed on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. It opens 9am to 1pm on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve, and 9am to 5pm on other days.

When do Slough schools break up for Christmas 2026?

Community and voluntary controlled schools finish on Friday 18 December 2026 and return on Monday 4 January 2027. Academies, free schools and voluntary aided schools can set different dates.
