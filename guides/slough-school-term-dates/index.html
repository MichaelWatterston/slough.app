Slough school term dates and holidays

Term dates, half terms and holidays for 2026/27 and 2027/28, plus INSET days, admissions deadlines and the reason your school's calendar might not match.

Last checked: 26 November 2026

The short version

  • These are Slough Borough Council's dates for community and voluntary controlled schools.
  • Academies, free schools and voluntary aided schools can set their own dates. Most Slough secondary schools are academies, so check your school's website before you book anything.
  • Every school also picks four of its own INSET days, when pupils stay at home.

INSET days in Slough

Schools have five INSET days a year. The council fixes one, at the very start of September. Each school chooses the other four and tells parents directly, which is why two children at different Slough schools can be off on different days. Look for the list on your school's website or in the first newsletter of the year.

Why your school's dates might be different

The council calendar only binds community and voluntary controlled schools. Academies, free schools and voluntary aided schools set their own, and in Slough that covers a large share of schools, including the grammar schools. Most stay close to the council pattern, but a school can finish a few days earlier in July or move a half term. Three places to check:

  • Your school's own term dates page, which is the only source that counts.
  • The council's school term dates page, for the community school calendar.
  • Other parents. School and year groups in the Slough App are a quick way to confirm an INSET day or an early finish.

Slough fun facts: school run edition

Four facts to try on the walk to school.

  • The first zebra crossing was painted in SloughOn 31 October 1951 the world's first zebra crossing was laid out in Slough, after trials by the Road Research Laboratory based in Langley. The road has since been pedestrianised, so the original stripes are gone.
  • Slough children sit one 11+ for four schoolsHerschel, Langley, Upton Court and St Bernard's Catholic grammar schools share a single entrance test, so a child applying to any of them only sits it once.
  • A school named after a planet finderHerschel Grammar School takes its name from William Herschel, the astronomer who discovered Uranus in 1781 and lived in Slough from 1786 until his death.
  • Britain's first salaried woman scientist worked hereCaroline Herschel, William's sister, discovered several comets from Slough. In 1787 King George III began paying her a salary for her astronomy work, a first for a woman in Britain.

Slough school holiday questions

When do Slough schools break up for Christmas 2026?

Community and voluntary controlled schools in Slough finish on Friday 18 December 2026 and return on Monday 4 January 2027. Academies, free schools and voluntary aided schools can set different dates.

When is February half term 2027 in Slough?

February half term runs from Monday 15 February to Friday 19 February 2027. Pupils return on Monday 22 February 2027.

When are the Easter holidays 2027 in Slough?

Term ends on Thursday 25 March 2027. The Easter holiday runs from Friday 26 March to Friday 9 April 2027, and pupils return on Monday 12 April 2027.

When is May half term 2027 in Slough?

May half term runs from Monday 31 May to Friday 4 June 2027.

When do Slough schools break up for summer 2027?

The last day of the summer term is Wednesday 21 July 2027 for community and voluntary controlled schools. The 2027/28 school year starts on Wednesday 1 September 2027, which is an INSET day.

Do all Slough schools have the same term dates?

No. Slough Borough Council's dates apply to community and voluntary controlled schools. Academies, free schools and voluntary aided schools can set their own term dates, and every school chooses four of its own INSET days, so always check with your school.

When is the deadline to apply for a primary school place in Slough for September 2027?

The deadline for Reception and Year 3 applications is 15 January 2027. Offers are made on 16 April 2027.
