Slough App is a community app built by people who live here, for the people who live here. One place for what's happening, what's for sale, who's hiring and who to meet.

Why we built it

When something opens up, gets sold or fills up in Slough, you should hear about it in time. Slough App keeps posts, listings, jobs and groups close to home, so what you see is relevant to your street, your schools, your shops and your neighbours.

What's inside

Local feed
Post updates, photos and videos to a feed that only Slough sees. Reply, react and stay in the loop with neighbours.
Buy and sell
List items in seconds, message buyers safely in-app and collect from somewhere you already know.
Jobs
Roles from Slough employers and side gigs from local residents, so you can find work without the commute.
Groups
Interest-based groups across 14 categories, including sport, families, food, faith, parenting and hobbies.

Free to use

Slough App is free to join and free to use, and every essential feature stays free. An optional Premium upgrade adds extra features and can be cancelled at any time in your device settings.

Looking after the community

We actively moderate content and respond quickly to reports. You can report or block anyone, hide keywords, topics or accounts you would rather not see, and delete your account and data at any time, no questions asked. You always have the final say over what you see.

Get in touch

Questions, ideas or feedback are always welcome. Visit the Help & Contact page to reach the team, or download the app and join your neighbours.
