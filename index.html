Getting started

Is Slough App free?

Yes. Slough App is free to join and use, and every essential feature stays free. Slough App Premium is an optional upgrade you can cancel at any time in your device settings.

Which phones does it work on?

Slough App is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

Do I need to live in Slough?

Slough App is built for people who live, work or spend time in Slough. Everything in the app, from posts to listings to groups, stays inside the town.

Using the app

How do I sell something?

Open For Sale, add a photo and a price, and your listing is live in under 30 seconds. Buyers message you safely in-app, you agree a spot in Slough to meet, and you can mark the item as sold when it goes.

How do I find local jobs?

The Jobs section lists roles from Slough employers and side gigs posted by local residents, so you can skip the commute and find work close to home.

How do groups work?

Groups are interest-based chats across 14 categories, including sport, families, food, faith, parenting and hobbies. Join the ones that match you and leave whenever you like.

Safety and data

How do I report a post or user?

Every post and message has a report option. A real team reviews reports quickly, and you can block any user instantly so they can't reach you.

Can I hide things I don't want to see?

Yes. You can mute keywords, topics or accounts, so you always have the final say over what appears in your feed.

How do I delete my account?

You can remove your account and all of your data at any time from within the app, no questions asked.
